# Fieldwork Documentation Notes

## Sampling-location Design

The field survey covered a 12-km route within Wadi Degla Protected Area.

Twenty-four predefined sampling locations were distributed at 0.5-km intervals along the study route.

The GPS record is organized under codes K1-K12, with two coordinate records associated with each code.

## Routine and Targeted Visits

Routine visits aimed to cover about 12 sampling locations during a field session. Field progression remained flexible when vegetation at a planned location was unsuitable for photographing or specimen collection.

Two visits followed a targeted collection strategy:

- 20 April 2024
- 7 February 2025

These visits focused on specific collection needs, including increasing representation of selected species and supporting botanical documentation.

## Image-acquisition Protocol

The field imaging strategy was designed to maximize visual diversity.

Plants were photographed from multiple viewpoints and distances, with whole-plant images and photographs of available organs such as leaves, flowers, fruits, and other visible structures.

The exact sequence of photographs depended on the condition of the plant and the organs available during the field encounter.

Multiple mobile devices were used during image acquisition.

## Botanical Verification Workflow

Botanical identification began with expert field identification.

Reference specimens representing the target species were collected during the fieldwork program and used alongside botanical expert review to confirm species identity.

Images associated with the confirmed field plants were assigned to the corresponding species within the curated field collection.

## Image Curation

A total of 300 field images were excluded during botanical verification and dataset curation because their species identity could not be established with sufficient confidence.

The final curated field collection contains 22,064 images representing 32 target plant species.

## Observation-level Interpretation

Field metadata were organized primarily at the species and collection-date levels.

Accordingly, image counts represent numbers of photographs and should not be interpreted as counts of independent plant individuals or independent biological observations.

## GPS Metadata

The field GPS record contains 24 coordinate records associated with the 24 predefined sampling locations.

The records are organized under codes K1-K12, with two coordinate records for each code.

The study route extended for 12 km, with sampling locations distributed at 0.5-km intervals.

The coordinate reference system/datum will be confirmed before production of the final study-area map.

## Field Permit

Field access and collection were conducted with permission from the relevant Egyptian environmental and protected-area authorities.

Detailed permit metadata will be incorporated after retrieval of the original permit record.
